# OSRS Wilderness multi-combat venue catalog

## Scope and confidence

**Verified** means supported by a current OSRS Wiki page or the RuneLite Wilderness Lines source at commit `d81bb4c444c3772b1563be8a8fcc2252c84a0a4f`. **Recommendation** is an operational judgment for a Clan War Board, not a game fact. The 2018 OSRS Advice image is useful historical orientation only; it should not define live boundaries because it predates singles-plus, the 2023 boss rework, and later map changes.

RuneLite's plugin models Wilderness multi as explicit `java.awt.Rectangle` world-coordinate areas and separately draws the overworld level-20 line at **y=3680** and level-30 line at **y=3760**. It also has underground line segments, filters underground geometry by plane/coordinate space, and checks its model against the live `MULTIWAY_INDICATOR` varbit. Therefore, coordinates below are strong boundary evidence, but the live crossed-swords indicator remains authoritative at event time. [RuneLite source](https://github.com/Nightfirecat/plugin-hub-plugins/blob/d81bb4c444c3772b1563be8a8fcc2252c84a0a4f/src/main/java/at/nightfirec/wildernesslines/WildernessLinesPlugin.java) · [RuneLite Plugin Hub](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub)

### Universal constraints (verified)

- Multi means multiple players/monsters may attack one target; the crossed-swords icon identifies it. Revenant Caves, Wilderness Agility Course, and the lesser Wilderness bosses are **singles-plus**, not multi. [OSRS Wiki: Multicombat](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Multicombat_area)
- Attackable combat-level range is your combat level ± current Wilderness level. Conventional teleportation stops above level 20; selected jewellery/seed-pod-style methods work through level 30. Tele Block normally lasts five minutes, halved with Protect from Magic, and minigame teleports do not work in the Wilderness. [OSRS Wiki: Wilderness](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Wilderness)
- RuneLite source rectangles use world X/Y; AWT rectangle upper bounds are exclusive. Region IDs below use `(x>>6, y>>6)` / packed region ID and are reference anchors, not a claim that the whole region is multi.
- **Plane rule:** surface and underground spaces can share apparent map geography but not coordinate Y or access. Never mix a surface KOTH marker with a cave marker. RuneLite's major underground envelope is `(3264,10048,192,320)` and WGWD is separately represented near y=10112–10176. [RuneLite source](https://github.com/Nightfirecat/plugin-hub-plugins/blob/d81bb4c444c3772b1563be8a8fcc2252c84a0a4f/src/main/java/at/nightfirec/wildernesslines/WildernessLinesPlugin.java)

Direct Annakarl teleport; otherwise approach from nearby deep-Wildy routes. RuneLite's broad north-east blocks include this area (`3152,3752,176×88`, anchor region 49,58 / **12602**). KOTH: A.** Classic large-team venue, recognizable ruins and defensible structures. RuneLite north-east blocks cover the deep NE wilderness (`3200,3904,192×64`, anchor region 50,61 / **12861**). Burning amulet to Lava Maze then south-west; Ghorrock then south; Cemetery then north; POH/Hard Diary obelisk to level 44 then south. Frequent PK hotspot. RuneLite exact rectangle `2946,3816,14×16`, anchor region 46,59 / **11835**. First floor reached via quest route is **safe and not Wilderness**, so exclude it. [Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Chaos_Temple_(church)) | **TDM: B. [Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Lava_Dragon_Isle) | **TDM: B+. KOTH: A-.** Natural island boundary and chokepoints are strong. ## Explicit exclusions / common traps

1. **Revenant Caves:** singles-plus, not multi; do not schedule a multi-only war there even though older maps/forums may suggest historical multi. Rev teleports now serve three entrances at levels 17, 26 and 40. [Multicombat](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Multicombat_area) · [Rev teleport](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Revenant_cave_teleport)
2. **Wilderness Agility Course:** current course is singles-plus (despite older lists mentioning a southern multi portion). Treat legacy 2018 images as stale. [Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Wilderness_Agility_Course)
3. **Artio, Spindel, Calvar’ion:** lesser bosses are singles-plus; only Callisto, Venenatis, Vet’ion are the multi versions. [Multicombat](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Multicombat_area)
4. **KBD lair, Corporeal Beast lair, Mage Arena bank, Ferox interior:** safe/non-Wilderness exceptions; their surface approaches may be dangerous or multi, but the interiors are not valid Wilderness multi venues. [Wilderness](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Wilderness)
5. **Chaos Temple upper floor:** safe and not Wilderness; ground floor is the valid multi venue. [Chaos Temple](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Chaos_Temple_(church))

## Board implementation recommendations

- Store each arena as `{name, plane, polygon/rectangles, wildy_min, wildy_max, ingress, exits, hazards, rules_version, verified_date}`; never only a location name.
- Require a pre-event referee walk with RuneLite **Wilderness Lines** and confirmation of the live crossed-swords icon on perimeter tiles. The plugin itself detects model/live-varbit mismatches, so neither a screenshot nor old static map should be treated as permanent truth. [RuneLite source](https://github.com/Nightfirecat/plugin-hub-plugins/blob/d81bb4c444c3772b1563be8a8fcc2252c84a0a4f/src/main/java/at/nightfirec/wildernesslines/WildernessLinesPlugin.java)
- For **TDM**, prioritize GDZ, Rogues’ Castle, Ferox apron, then Dark Warriors’ Fortress. For **KOTH**, prioritize GDZ ruins, Rogues’ courtyard, west Chaos Altar, or a low-level obelisk 3×3.
- Record legality for NPC damage, altar/prayer use, obelisk activation, agility shortcuts, doors/webs, leaving the polygon, teleporting, and outside interference.
- Combat-level eligibility must be checked against the shallowest tile in the arena; do not assume a deep-Wildy roster remains mutually attackable at level 11–19 venues.
